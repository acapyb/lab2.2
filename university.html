Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah

Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA) is a public technical university located in Pahang, Malaysia.

UMPSA offers various programmes in engineering, technology, computing and other fields. The university has campuses in Pekan and Gambang.

I am currently studying at UMPSA Pekan Campus in the field of Computer Systems and Networking.
